# Health

A **Health** tab on the control panel (next to My Day) with five parts: **Exercise**, **Diet**, **Heart**,
**Sleep** and **Weight**. Each has a 7-day summary, a 14-day chart, a goal, and its log.

## You

At the top of the Health tab, **You**: your age, sex (optional), height, fitness level (from "just starting"
to "athlete"), how active you are day to day, and any injuries, conditions or limits. Every plan matches your
age and fitness level and works around your limits; with no profile, plans stay gentle. Max also works out
your BMI (height and latest weight) and heart-rate zones for your age, and includes them in "how am I doing?".
Your age goes up by itself each year.

## Logging

On the control panel (**Add** in each part), or just tell Max:

- "I ran 5 km in 30 minutes", "I did 40 minutes of weights yesterday"
- "I had porridge for breakfast, about 350 calories", "I drank 2 litres of water"
- "My blood pressure is 128 over 82", "resting heart rate 62"
- "I slept 7 hours", "I weigh 81.2 kilos"

Ask "how am I doing this week?" or "what's my blood pressure been like?". A very high blood pressure or an
unusual resting heart rate comes with a nudge to measure again and to get advice (Max isn't a doctor).

## Plans: exercise routines and meal plans

In **Health → Plans**, create a plan:

- **Websites to follow** (up to 5): a training programme, a page of exercises or recipes, or a whole list of
  them (e.g. `menshealth.com/uk/workouts`). A list is followed: Max picks the workouts that fit your brief
  (4 for a day's plan, 7 for a week's), different ones from the last few plans, reads each and builds the
  plan from them, naming where each idea comes from. The plan shows which pages it was built from. With
  Firecrawl set up (Connections → Web search) Max reads sites that block simple downloads (about 5
  Firecrawl credits a plan); without it he reads them directly where the site allows.
- **What you want**: your level, goal, equipment, time you have, injuries or foods to avoid.
- **Covers** the day or the week ahead; **when** it's made and sent (days and time).
- **Send** by email (to the address you set, from your connected Gmail or Outlook) and/or WhatsApp (to your
  own number, "Message yourself"). Plans only ever go to you.

Max takes your recent log into account (progressing gently, not repeating yesterday's hard session) and
varies each plan from the last. Ask "what's my workout today?", or "make me a new workout" for a fresh one.

The plans are written by the model chosen in **Settings → AI models → Health plans** (e.g. DeepSeek); until
you choose one, Max's main model writes them. A "thinking" model (e.g. DeepSeek's reasoner) gets plenty of room to think
and still answer; if a model gives back nothing, Max's main model writes that plan instead, and the log says why. With Firecrawl set up (Connections → Web search), Max can read
more websites (ones that need a browser).

## Weekly meals and the shopping list

1. **Health → You**: set your **goal** (lose weight, build muscle, keep my weight, eat healthily). With your age,
   height, weight and activity, Max works out your daily calories and protein, and splits them across the meals.
2. **Health → Dietary requirements**: add everyone who eats (e.g. you and Magda): their diet (vegetarian, vegan,
   pescatarian, or none), allergies (never used), and other needs or dislikes. The number of people sets the portions.
3. **Health → Plans → New plan**: "Meal plan", covering "the week ahead", with lunch and dinner ticked (and
   breakfast if you like), recipe websites if you have favourites, the day and time it's made (e.g. Sunday 17:00),
   and how it's sent.

Max writes the week's recipes (ingredients with quantities, steps, time, calories and protein each), practical
for a busy home (weekday dinners within 40 minutes, lunches from the previous dinner's leftovers, ingredients
reused to cut waste) and one shopping list for the week, by supermarket aisle.

**Every recipe is checked by Max himself** against everyone's requirements: meat, fish or seafood (and stock,
gelatine, Worcestershire sauce) in a vegetarian meal, animal products in a vegan one, allergens, and the cheeses
made with animal rennet (Parmesan, Pecorino, Grana Padano, Gorgonzola: a vegetarian hard cheese instead). A slip
is sent back to the AI to fix; anything still wrong is shown on the plan as "Please check".

- **Email**: the whole week, every recipe and the shopping list. **WhatsApp**: the week at a glance and the
  shopping list, and (if ticked) **each morning that day's recipes**.
- **Health → This week's meals**: every recipe, and the shopping list to tick off in the shop on your phone.
- Ask Max: "what's for dinner tonight?" (he puts the recipe on his screen, step by step), "what's for lunch
  tomorrow?", "what's on this week?", "what do I still need to buy?", "add oat milk to the shopping list",
  "I've got the spinach". Things you add yourself stay on the list when the next week's plan arrives.

### Adding ingredients, and shopping online

- **🛒 Add to the shopping list**: on the meal plan, "Add all the week's ingredients" (or under each recipe,
  "Add these ingredients"). Things already on the list aren't added twice. By voice: "add tonight's dinner
  ingredients to the shopping list". A new meal plan also fills the list by itself.
- **I shop at**: Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Morrisons, Waitrose, Ocado, Iceland or Amazon Fresh. Each item gets a
  **🔍 link** to that supermarket's search for it ("2 × 280 g firm tofu" searches for "firm tofu").
- **Find products** (needs Firecrawl, about one credit per item): Max reads the supermarket's results and lists
  the products with prices; **Choose** one and next week Max suggests the same ("Last time").
- Max can't put things in your basket: supermarkets don't allow it (it would mean logging in as you). Tap a
  product to open it on the supermarket's site and add it there.

## Privacy

Health records are personal: kept on the box, encrypted with Max's memory key, never in the logs, and only
available in personal mode (not in guest or work mode). Plans are sent only to you, and never from a work
account.

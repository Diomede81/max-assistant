# Journal: your days, and Max's reviews of them

A **Journal** area on the control panel (and the `journal` action by voice): what you did, day by day,
in your own words, with what Max adds himself, and a weekly and monthly review of your life written
from it. Personal: kept encrypted on the box; Max uses it by voice in personal mode only (the control
panel shows it in personal and work mode, never in guest mode).

## Telling Max what you did

Any time of day: "I went to the gym and had lunch with Marco", "just back from the dentist", "rough day
at work", "yesterday I finally fixed the bike". Max notes it with the time; say how the day felt
("it was a good day", "felt great") and he keeps a mood from rough to great. On the control panel you
can type an entry, pick a mood, and add to earlier days.

## What Max adds by himself

- **Your calendar events, personal and work**, read each evening (default 21:00) straight from every
  connected calendar (Connections → Apps: the personal set and the work set), one line per event, work
  ones marked "Work event". Read whatever mode Max is in at that hour: the work calendar is read even
  though work mode is over by the evening. Nothing is left to the AI to find: Max calls the calendar's own
  "view a day" action, and the AI only tidies the answer into lines.
- **Meetings he joined** for you (Meetings): title, how long, and the gist once he has it.

Both can be switched off under Journal → Settings.

## The reviews

Every week (default Sunday 19:00) and on the last day of each month, Max writes a review: the week in
short, what you did, people, health, work, how it felt, patterns he noticed, and a few gentle questions
for the next week. He writes it from the journal, your health numbers, the to-dos you finished and the
people you were in touch with; he never invents what isn't there. Reviews stay on the control panel
(Journal → Reviews) and can be emailed or sent to you on WhatsApp. Ask "how was my week?" and Max goes
through it with you; "write my review now" makes a fresh one.

The model that writes them is chosen under Settings → AI models → Life reviews.

| You say | Max does |
| --- | --- |
| "I went for a run, then worked from home; dinner with Anna" | Notes it for today |
| "Yesterday was rough" | Notes the mood for yesterday |
| "What did I do today?" / "What did I do this week?" | Reads the journal back |
| "How was my week?" / "How was my month?" | Goes through the latest review |
| "Write my weekly review now" | Writes a fresh one (takes a minute) |

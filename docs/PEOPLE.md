# People: keeping in touch

A **People** area on the control panel (and the `people` action by voice) for the people close to you:
who they are, when you were last in touch, what they're into and how they're doing, with a nudge when
it's been too long. Personal: kept encrypted on the box. Max only uses it by voice in personal mode (never for guests or at
work); the control panel shows it in personal and work mode, never in guest mode.

## Each person

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Circle | **Family**, **Close friend**, **Friend**, **Colleague** or **Acquaintance**. Each has a default for how often you'd like to be in touch (7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days); change it per person |
| Relation | "my sister", "old school friend" |
| In touch | Every time you spoke, called, texted, met or emailed, with the date and anything they told you. Logged by voice, on the control panel (one tap: Spoke / Texted / Met), or noticed from WhatsApp: a message to or from their number, in a chat Max may see, counts as texting |
| Interests | What they're into, for gifts and things to talk about |
| How they are | A dated note each time there's news: a new job, a knee on the mend, a holiday |
| Birthday | Shown ahead of time, on Home when it's within three days |

## By voice

| You say | Max does |
| --- | --- |
| "Add my sister Anna to my people" / "Add Marco, a close friend" | Adds them with the right circle |
| "I called Mum, her knee is better" | Logs the call today, notes the news |
| "Had lunch with Marco on Saturday" | Logs that you met, on that date |
| "Anna's into climbing now" | Adds an interest |
| "What's new with Marco?" | Everything noted: circle, last contact, interests, news |
| "Who should I catch up with?" | Who it has been too long with, and birthdays coming |
| "Remind me about Marco next week" | No reminder about Marco for 7 days |

Max knows the list in every personal-mode conversation, so he logs contact when you mention it, notes
news you share, and can gently suggest a catch-up. The daily briefing ("what's going on in my day?")
mentions who's worth catching up with and birthdays in the next week. On Home, **Needs your attention**
lists overdue catch-ups and birthdays within three days.

## Reminders

Once a day (18:00 unless you change it, People › Reminders), when it's been too long with someone, Max
says so out loud ("It's been a while: 20 days since you called Mum, and 35 since you texted Marco. Last
you heard, Mum's knee was on the mend. Worth a call?") and shows them on his screen, each with an
**In touch** button (logs that you spoke today) and **Later**, which pauses the reminder for everyone
on the card for a few days (3 unless you change it). Only in personal mode, never for guests, and never
while you're talking to him or using a card.

By voice, "remind me about Marco next week" pauses just Marco. On the control panel, **Later** next to an
overdue person does the same. Switch the spoken or on-screen reminder off on the People page; the
Home page and the daily briefing still list who's overdue.

## WhatsApp

With a phone number on a person, Max fills in "last texted" from WhatsApp by himself: the last message to
or from that number, in a chat he may see (your own chat, and the chats allowed under Connections →
WhatsApp). He never reads chats you haven't allowed.

Printed parts

    Measure before printing

    1. Screen: glass diameter, visible diameter and the size of the module behind it.
    2. Mic board: diameter and mounting-hole positions.
    3. Speaker: spacing of the 4 mounting holes.

    These are best estimates until the parts arrive. Each is one setting at the top of the design file.
